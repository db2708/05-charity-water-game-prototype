Instructions
  1. Move the jerrycan with with your arrow keys or by clicking. Click inside the well or press SPACE to drop the current drop.
  2. When two water drops of the same size and color touch, they merge into the next largest drop.
  3. If the well fills and a drop sits above the top boundary, the game ends.
Scoring: merges award points (bigger merges = more points).
